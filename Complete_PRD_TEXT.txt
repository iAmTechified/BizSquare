Product Requirements Document (PRD)
WhatsApp-first Business Growth Platform
Version 1.0
Executive Summary
A platform for intelligent networking, dynamic contact management, visibility management and lightweight CRM built around WhatsApp.
Vision
Help small businesses acquire customers, manage relationships and grow from one application.
Problem Statement
Manual Contact Gain creates spam, poor leads, competitor overlap, stale contacts and no measurable growth.
Goals
Increase quality networking, automate contacts, improve visibility, provide business tools and measurable growth.
Target Users
Small businesses, freelancers, students, vendors, creators and communities.
User Journey
Register → Profile → Business → Interests → Smart Matches → Sync Contacts → Spotlight → CRM → Campaigns → Analytics.
Core Modules
Authentication, Business Profiles, Smart Networking, Contact Management, Pocket CRM, Visibility Management, Status Akawo, WhatsApp Tools, Business Toolkit, Growth, Trust Center, Delivery, Analytics and Admin Dashboard.
Key Contact Features
Auto sync, cleanup, labels, groups, stars, archive, notes, lead grading, relationship strength, interaction score, analytics, audit trail.
WhatsApp Features
Status scheduling, broadcast scheduling, catalog manager, click-to-chat, QR codes, quick replies.
Business Features
Invoices, receipts, quotations, payment links, campaigns, UGC, affiliates, ads, content testing.
Admin Dashboard
Manage users, spotlight rotation, overrides, matching, reports, verification, ads, analytics and configuration.
Business Rules
Reciprocal matching, no competitor matching, fair distribution, contact caps, fallback logic, dynamic contacts, rotating spotlight.
Functional Requirements
Users manage businesses, receive matches, sync contacts, manage CRM, schedule campaigns; admins manage platform.
Non-functional Requirements
Secure, scalable, reliable, privacy-first and responsive.
MVP
Profiles, matching, contacts, CRM basics, spotlight, analytics, admin dashboard, invoices, catalog, payment links.
Future
AI, automation, chatbot, POS, loyalty.
