BizSquare Brand & Design Style Guide
Version 1.0
Brand Identity
Brand Name: BizSquare
Tagline (working): Where Businesses Grow Together.
Brand Personality: Modern, confident, approachable, optimistic, community-driven, trustworthy.
Brand Positioning
BizSquare is a WhatsApp-first Business Growth Platform that helps SMEs grow through smart networking, visibility management, contact intelligence, lightweight CRM, and business tools.
Design Principles
• Simplicity over clutter
• Human-first experiences
• Business-friendly, not corporate
• Clean and consistent layouts
• Fast, intuitive interactions
• Trust through clarity
• Focus on growth and visibility
Visual Style
Design Language:
- Soft Minimalism
- Rounded corners (12–20px)
- Large cards
- Spacious layouts
- Minimal shadows
- Subtle micro-animations
- Consistent iconography

Typography
Primary: Inter
Alternative: Manrope
Headings: SemiBold/Bold
Body: Regular
Use generous spacing and avoid dense text.
Icons & Illustrations
Icons: Lucide-style rounded outline icons.
Illustrations: Flat vector illustrations featuring African entrepreneurs, online sellers, customers, logistics and collaboration. Avoid overly cartoonish artwork.
UI Components
Buttons: Rounded, medium height, clear hierarchy.
Cards: Elevated with soft shadows.
Forms: Clean inputs with descriptive labels.
Charts: Simple, business-focused analytics.
Navigation: Bottom navigation (mobile), sidebar (web admin).
Experience
The app should feel calm, premium, encouraging and productive. Every screen should reinforce business growth, trust and momentum rather than complexity.
Brand Voice
Friendly, clear and encouraging.
Examples:
✓ 'Your campaign is ready.'
✓ 'You gained 3 new business contacts today.'
✓ 'Your visibility increased this week.'
Avoid robotic or overly technical language.
Logo Direction
Modern wordmark emphasizing 'Square' as a place where businesses meet and grow. Explore subtle square/grid motifs representing community, marketplace and opportunity. Avoid chat bubbles or WhatsApp-inspired logos.
