Functional Specification Document (FSD)
WhatsApp-first Business Growth Platform
Version 1.0
1. System Overview
The platform consists of a Flutter mobile application, backend services, and a web-based admin dashboard. The mobile app focuses on networking, CRM, visibility, and business management while the admin dashboard manages operations and analytics.

3. Authentication
Functions:
- Register/Login
- OTP Verification
- Password Reset
- Session Management
- Logout
Validation:
- Unique phone number
- Valid OTP
- Required profile information
4. Business Profiles
Users can create one or more businesses.
Fields include business name, category, description, location, contact details, social links, catalog and business status.
5. Smart Match Engine
