Product Requirements Document (Draft)
Product Vision
Build a mobile platform that intelligently connects businesses and professionals through reciprocal, high-quality WhatsApp networking, replacing manual Contact Gain with fair, dynamic, and automated relationship management.
Problem Statement
Current Contact Gain methods create spam, competitor overlap, poor lead quality, stale contacts, and no intelligent matching.
Solution
A mobile app that registers users, understands interests, intelligently matches people, dynamically syncs contacts, rotates promotional campaigns (Status Akawo), and maintains a healthy networking ecosystem.
Core Principles
• Mutual reciprocity
• No competitor matching
• Fair exposure
• Dynamic contacts
• Low friction
• Privacy and trust
Target Users
Small business owners, freelancers, students, professionals, creators, cooperatives, and community groups.
Core Features
• Registration & onboarding
• Business profiles
• Intelligent matchmaking
• Contact sync & cleanup
• Status Akawo rotation
• Search & discovery
• Notifications
• Analytics
• Admin dashboard
User Journey
Register → Complete profile → Select interests → Receive curated matches → Sync contacts → Participate in Status Akawo → Build relationships → Measure results.
Matching Rules
1. Reciprocal matches.
2. Never match competitors.
3. Contact cap per batch.
4. Fair load balancing.
5. Fallback matching when interests are unavailable.
6. Remove inactive users from future syncs.
Modules
Authentication, Profiles, Matching Engine, Contact Engine, Status Akawo, Discovery, Reputation, Notifications, Analytics, Admin.
SOPs
Daily: Generate matches, rotate Akawo, process reports.
Weekly: Refresh contacts and clean inactive users.
Monthly: Review analytics, rebalance categories, audit abuse.
Success Metrics
Activation, retained users, accepted matches, contact quality, Akawo participation, business enquiries, user retention.
